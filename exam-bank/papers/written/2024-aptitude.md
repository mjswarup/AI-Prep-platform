# Previous Year Written Paper - Aptitude & Verbal

## Quantitative Aptitude

1. A train travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find the speed in km/hr.
2. A shopkeeper marks an item 25% above cost price and gives a 10% discount. What is the profit percentage?
3. The average of 5 consecutive numbers is 18. Find the largest number.
4. If 12 men can finish a work in 10 days, how many days will 8 men take?
5. A compound interest problem involving principal, rate, and time.

## Logical Reasoning

6. Find the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
7. In a seating arrangement question, determine who sits opposite to whom.
8. Solve a blood-relation puzzle.
9. Identify the odd one out among patterns or directions.
10. Data sufficiency question based on marks and percentages.

## Verbal Ability

11. Choose the correct word to complete the sentence.
12. Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
13. Write a short paragraph on "The importance of communication in campus placements".
14. Synonym and antonym-based MCQ set.
15. Reading comprehension with 3 questions.

## Expected focus
- Arithmetic and percentages
- Ratio, profit-loss, time-speed-distance
- Direction sense and seating puzzles
- Grammar and sentence correction
- Short essay writing
